# Time — users jump

Dominant when they default, impulse-buy, abandon, or take the shortcut.

### Labor Illusion
Visible work raises perceived quality (KAYAK effect).
**Do:** “searching 120 flights” when you actually are.
**Don't:** Tinder-instant matches that feel cheap; lying ETAs.

### Default Bias
Status quo unless the incentive to change is strong.
**Do:** green default (Uber Eats: no disposables).
**Don't:** subscribe-as-default, zombie Adobe renewals, hidden cancel.

### Investment Loops
Time/data/effort in → next trigger loaded (Eyal).
**Do:** Strava goals, saved prefs that make tomorrow better.
**Don't:** hostage data with no export.

### Loss Aversion
Losses hurt more than equal gains (Kahneman & Tversky).
**Do:** Duolingo gems at risk of a streak they chose.
**Don't:** “upgrade or lose your data” as a hostage.

### Commitment & Consistency
Small yes → later yes (Cialdini; micro-commitments).
**Do:** one question per step they mean.
**Don't:** a meaningless yes used to trap identity on cancel.

### Sunk Cost Effect
Past spend keeps them in. Fallacy when it won’t make them happier.
**Do:** let them leave a bad movie.
**Don't:** “you’ve already invested 14 days.”

### Reactance
Force creates the opposite (Brehm).
**Do:** visible exit on a trial paywall (Blinkist lift).
**Don't:** YouTube-ad blitz to force Premium; notification storms.

### Law of the Instrument
Hammer → nails (Maslow).
**Do:** ask “am I using the right tool?”
**Don't:** gamify every job because you shipped points.

### Temptation Bundling
Want + should together (Milkman).
**Do:** pair the hard setup with something they already enjoy.
**Don't:** hide the should inside an addictive want.

### Dunning-Kruger Effect
Novices overrate skill; experts underrate relative skill.
**Do:** data + named principles when a HIPPO overrides.
**Don't:** treat intuition as evidence.

### Discoverability
They can extract the next move from the screen (Norman).
**Do:** back path, mic-on state, visible primary.
**Don't:** Clubhouse-class “is my mic on?”

### Second-Order Effect
Consequences of the consequences (cobra effect).
**Do:** name the side effect before you ship the loop.
**Don't:** optimize shares until people share unread links.

### Decision Fatigue
Many prior choices → sloppier next one. Not a glucose tank (replications failed).
**Do:** fewer decisions, especially late; smart defaults.
**Don't:** dump the annual upsell at the end of a 12-step wizard.

### Observer-Expectancy Effect
Researcher leaks the hypothesis (Rosenthal).
**Do:** script tasks, blind where you can, preregister.
**Don't:** “try the obvious upgrade” in a usability script.

### Weber's Law
JND is a *ratio* of the baseline (Weber–Fechner).
**Do:** ship UI in small steps; make real improvements clear the JND.
**Don't:** shrinkflation / quiet price creep designed to stay unseen.

### Parkinson's Law
Work fills the time allowed (1955).
**Do:** “2-minute setup” that is actually 2 minutes; timeboxed first run.
**Don't:** resetting countdown urgency.

### Affect Heuristic
Good/bad tag stands in for risk/benefit (Slovic).
**Do:** warm brand *and* a readable fee table.
**Don't:** cute UX on a predatory loan; fear-colored VPN upsell.

### Hyperbolic Discounting
Now >> later; preference reverses (Ainslie; Laibson).
**Do:** commitment devices they choose; show future cost in present tense.
**Don't:** BNPL / trial-convert-unless-cancel at the weak moment.

### Chronoception
Felt time ≠ clock (Maister waiting lines).
**Do:** occupy the wait; start the service; determinate progress.
**Don't:** looping “30 seconds” bars.

### Cashless Effect
Invisible money → more spend (Hirschman; Soman; Prelec).
**Do:** running total, “you’re about to spend $48.”
**Don't:** in-app currency that hides the meter, especially from kids.

### Self-serving bias
Credit the win, blame the app (Miller & Ross).
**Do:** errors without shame; dashboards that don’t launder product failure as “users.”
**Don't:** “invalid user.”

### Pareto Principle
Vital few drive most effect (Juran). Not a bias. Not always 80/20.
**Do:** instrument top tasks / top errors first.
**Don't:** delete the long-tail path new users need.

### Backfire Effect
Correction makes the myth stronger. **Rare.** Usually they just ignore you.
**Do:** correct plainly; give a replacement story.
**Don't:** refuse to correct, or identity-attack (“you’re dumb for believing”).

### False Consensus Effect
“Everyone thinks like me” (Ross, Greene, House 1977).
**Do:** talk to people who are not the team.
**Don't:** ship dark mode + 12 shortcuts because you wanted them.

### Bandwagon Effect
Adoption begets adoption (Leibenstein).
**Do:** true trending / waitlists.
**Don't:** fake counters. Distinct from social proof (heuristic vs dynamic).

### Barnum-Forer Effect
Generic flattering copy feels personal (Forer 1949).
**Do:** Wrapped with real data.
**Don't:** $99 “personality” that is a horoscope.

### IKEA Effect
Labor that *finishes* raises value (Norton, Mochon, Ariely 2012).
**Do:** name the board, finish a setup they keep.
**Don't:** unpaid labor as “customization”; destroy-on-leave.

### Planning Fallacy
This project will be faster than the last ones (Kahneman & Tversky; Buehler).
**Do:** outside view / reference class; ranges not “2-minute setup.”
**Don't:** knowingly impossible “done in a minute” hostage flows.
